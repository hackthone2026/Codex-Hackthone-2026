Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Quick answers regarding Udyam registrations, credit subsidies, and application status.

How can I check the live status of my loan or MSME application?
You can track your submitted requests in real-time under the Loan Services or MSME Services sections where live ledger statuses (e.g., "Under Review", "Certificate Issued") are updated automatically.
What documents are mandatory for Udyam MSME registration?
You only need an Aadhaar card linked with your active mobile number, your enterprise/proprietor PAN card, bank account details, and plant address pincode. No paper uploads are required.
How do I claim the Credit Linked Capital Subsidy (CLCSS)?
Micro and small enterprises acquiring approved machinery through term loans can apply through the DC-MSME portal or our MSME Subsidies application drawer.

Platform Architecture & Features

CODEX is a synchronized MSME dashboard and database management platform. Key modules include:

  • Dashboard & Analytics: Real-time charts powered by Chart.js tracking sales velocity, revenue targets, and performance logs.
  • eCommerce & Inventory: Complete order tracking, product catalogs, customer management, and A4 print export tools.
  • Loan & MSME Services: Integrated Udyam registration guides, 40+ government scheme vaults, EMI smart calculators, and eligibility evaluators.
  • Kalnirnay Calendar: Traditional Indian calendar view integrated with GST compliance deadlines and loan EMI due dates.

Local Storage Database Schema

The platform utilizes browser localStorage under the database key CODEX_LOCAL_DATABASE_V42. It handles persistence for:

  • Profiles & Accounts: Synchronized user data, social handles, avatars, and Supabase host authentication credentials.
  • Dynamic Currency Engine: Real-time calculations across INR (₹), USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), and JPY (¥).
  • Activity Notification Logs: Automated event tracking for order placements, supply fulfillments, and customer updates.
  • OpenAI GPT Integration: Connects local database states directly to GPT-4o for natural language querying via the floating chat orb.

Complete Udyam MSME Registration Guide

Zero Govt Fee • Paperless

Official Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises guidelines, classifications, and walkthrough.

Composite Classification Criteria

Micro Enterprise

Investment: Up to ₹ 1 Crore

Turnover: Up to ₹ 5 Crore

Small Enterprise

Investment: Up to ₹ 10 Crore

Turnover: Up to ₹ 50 Crore

Medium Enterprise

Investment: Up to ₹ 50 Crore

Turnover: Up to ₹ 250 Crore

Mandatory Documents & Details

Aadhaar Card: Linked with mobile for OTP
PAN Card: Enterprise or proprietor's PAN
Bank Account: Current or savings IFSC code
NIC 2/4 Digit Code: Primary business activity
GSTIN (if applicable): Auto-verified via CBDT
Plant / Location Address: PIN code & unit count

Step-by-Step Registration Walkthrough

Step 1
Aadhaar & OTP Verification

Visit udyamregistration.gov.in. Select "For New Entrepreneurs" and authenticate via Aadhaar OTP.

Step 2
PAN Validation

Enter your PAN number. The portal automatically extracts and validates ITR filing data from income tax databases.

Step 3
Plant Location & NIC Code Selection

Add all factory/office physical addresses and pick your National Industry Classification (NIC) manufacturing or service code.

Step 4
Machinery Investment & Final OTP

Enter written-down investment cost in plant & machinery and submit the final mobile verification OTP.

Step 5
Instant Udyam Registration Number

A lifetime valid 19-digit URN (e.g., UDYAM-MH-12-0012345) and QR-code enabled certificate is issued within 24–48 hours.
